How to Play

The rules behind this board
🛫
Takeoff Roll a 6 to leave the hangar for the ready pad. Any roll after that launches you onto the board.
🎯
Own-color bonus Land on a cell matching your color and you auto-advance 4 more cells — mandatory, every time.
⛽
Gas stations Reach one and choose: fly the shortcut (capturing anything in your path) or take the normal route.
💥
Capturing Land on an opponent's plane on an unsafe cell and it's sent back to their hangar.
🏠
Coming home You need the exact roll to land on cell 6 of your home stretch. Overshoot and you bounce back the remaining steps.
🏆
Winning First to get all 4 planes home wins the race.
